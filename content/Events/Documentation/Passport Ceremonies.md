---
title: "Passport Ceremonies"
---

Passport ceremonies are typically a \~1 hour event where approximately 10 people will create a passport during [Hack Night](https://www.notion.so/282181f3b6ed80dea06dc05d08e8e8e4). This event takes the place of a [Session](https://www.notion.so/309181f3b6ed8185b638d5f1ff1b62f2) every two weeks. See [Passports](https://www.notion.so/309181f3b6ed8144aa05da076109e291) for more information about the physical passport.
## Preparation
Two weeks before the event, make [an announcement](https://discord.com/channels/772576325897945119/809620019792707594/1283602036683837546) in the Discord server to let everyone know that the Passport Ceremony will be happening at the next Hack Night!
Add a new ceremony date that is scheduled for the next Hack Night on the [Passports Issuing Office](https://passports.purduehackers.com/admin) and ensure that the date is visible on the [Passports](https://passports.purduehackers.com) website. Note, you will need to be an admin Passport holder to access the admin page (see [Passports](https://www.notion.so/309181f3b6ed8144aa05da076109e291)).
![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/purduehackers/evergreen/main/events/passport-ceremonies/images/passports-admin-dashboard.png)
To prepare materials for a passport ceremony, reference [Prep](https://www.notion.so/309181f3b6ed810c9841eb1e5b769ca8). This should be done after you get the target number of participants for the event. Be mindful to do this well before the event, ideally two-three days before.
## Day of Event
Ensure that all materials for the Passport Ceremony are placed in the [Hack Cart](https://www.notion.so/309181f3b6ed815d987ddfe461d17979) prior to Hack Night starting. The organizer bringing the Hack Cart should ensure the Passport materials are in the cart.
### Setup
Follow the steps in [Setup](https://www.notion.so/309181f3b6ed81dbab6ecbdc16a0619d) to set up the kitchen and the stations for the event.
### During the Event
Gather all participants at 10:00 PM to start the event. Explain to them the process of creating a passport and pass around a few demo passports to show them what they will be creating.
Provide them with their passport bundles and follow the steps in [Event](https://www.notion.so/309181f3b6ed8141b3e2eada1ae60288) to guide them through the process of creating their passport. Individuals will be working at their own pace, so ensure that organizers are available to help participants at each station.
### After the Event
Ensure that everyone's passport has been activated. If not, follow the steps in [Event \> Activation](https://www.notion.so/309181f3b6ed8141b3e2eada1ae60288) to activate the passport. Once all passports have been activated, place them under a heavy surface (e.g. some books) and tell participants to wait for one hour (or until they leave).
Ensure all passports are retrieved before the end of the night. If a participant leaves their passport behind, place it on the Hack Cart for them to pick up at a later date.
Follow the steps in [Cleanup](https://www.notion.so/309181f3b6ed81f99599fa929974f949) to clean up the kitchen and the stations after the event.
