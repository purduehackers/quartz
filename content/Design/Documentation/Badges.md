## Overview
Every week for Hack Night, the Design team creates a new laser-cut acrylic badge and rubber stamp. The badge is a collectible that attendees can take home. This section documents everything we’ve learned about the process.
---
## Key Facts
- **Material**: Typically 1/8" acrylic from [Canal Plastics](https://www.canalplastic.com/)
- **Ideal size**: 2.0–2.7 inches per dimension
- **Yield**: 40–50 badges per 12x18 sheet
- **Software**: Any vector editor that exports SVG (Illustrator, Inkscape, Figma)
- **Colors**: Black = engrave, Red (#FF0000) = cut, White = no engrave

## DPI Settings for Badge Engraving
The laser cutter's engraving DPI (dots per inch) can be adjusted for different levels of detail:
- Default: 300 DPI — good for most badge designs
- Sweet spot: 600 DPI — optimal balance of quality and speed for most badges
- High detail: Up to 1000 DPI — best for highly detailed engravings like Prisha's badge
Important: Engraving time scales directly with DPI. Higher DPI settings will take significantly longer to complete but produce sharper, more detailed results.
When to adjust DPI:
- Use 300 DPI for simple designs with basic text and shapes
- Use 600 DPI for most detailed badge designs
- Use 1000 DPI only for badges with very fine details or intricate patterns that require maximum sharpness
