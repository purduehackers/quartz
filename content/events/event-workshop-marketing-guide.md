<callout icon="✅" color="yellow_bg">
	Guidelines and ideas for marketing Purdue Hackers events and workshops.
</callout>
For **workshops**, use the **Project Template in the Events Linear team**. Most of the items below are transcribed from that template.
### RSVP Tracking
We use **Luma** to track RSVPs. As always, username and password in Vaultwarden.
Add a `utm_source=` parameter to the Luma link in each channel so we can trace sign-ups.
- Example: `https://luma.com/your-event?utm_source=cs361`
- Common sources: `discord`, `newsletter`, `instagram`, `posters`, `cs361`, `datamine`.
### Marketing Things (in order of how early they should be done)
- Make the Luma link.
- Email CS advisors for big events — we should use infrequently but for big ticket items.
- Email Patricia for CS Opportunities Newsletter feature. CS Newsletter is released every Wednesday during the semester. They *don’t need a poster.*
- **Create a poster! **This will be used in most other marketing channels.
- Draft a short “core blurb” (1–2 sentences) that every marketing channel will use.
- Email professors teaching relevant classes. *See example at end.*
- Email Datamine for datamine feature if appropriate. *See example at end.*
- Put up posters around campus!
- Discord announcement. See examples in Discord.
- Create an Instagram post.
- Email blast all-previous event attendees —- assemble the list from Luma signups for past workshops.
