Don’t make it stretch from May to March next year next time.
# SIGHORSE 2026 Planning
- Why SIGHORSE?
	- The community gets a cool event over the summer.
	- The club gets documentation of cool projects that people made. People get reviews, motivation and help completing their summer projects.
	- Club sponsors see cool things we do. Alumni and members of the community enjoy consuming SIGHORSE.
- There should be a page limit (thanks Arhan!)
- There should be a more concrete expectation for articles; each article should *educate* the reader in at least some way, *or *focus on being really really funny (Estrogen is All You Need was a highlight!!)
- **Should not run from May to March and overlap with Commit Overflow.** Run May to August.
- We need a more concrete system for reviews; make authors review other papers.
- *People need pings to make progress*; we should set up a system to help people make progress.
- We should have a standardized Typst template that people can use as a jumping off point —- SIGHORSE shouldn’t require people to learn Typst, and we *should* allow people to modify their Typst template as necessary.
- Arhan asks:* “How can we change SIGHORSE this year? Running it with the same name, same branding, same everything doesn’t seem very interesting.”*
- We need a larger organizational team than just one person.
- We need to market SIGHORSE earlier and more proactively to a larger audience than just Purdue Hackers. Maybe reach out to other clubs.
- Consider giving people an early-exit option: most projects started and fell-off / died midway. We should give those projects a way to still get into SIGHORSE in a reduced scope that still preserves quality —- maybe allow them to get a single page into SIGHORSE that’s still polished and still delivers.
## List of things to do:
- Market SIGHORSE ahead of time.
