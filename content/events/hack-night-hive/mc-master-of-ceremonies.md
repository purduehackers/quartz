---
title: "MC (Master of Ceremonies)"
---

The MC role has several moments where they need to speak about kickoff, sessions, circles, sponsors, countdown, and checkpoints. The following sections are listed in order and at each time what to announce.
## Kickoff
At kickoff which happens either at **8:30 **or **8:45 PM** kickoff must be announced. Prior to this recruit other organizers to help bring students downstairs. Then, announce the following items in order, note if it is not relevant to the current hack night then skip it.
1. Announce any sponsors. If there is a sponsor for the current hack night announce who they are, that they are sponsoring the current hack night. If they have a sponsor message be sure to read that. If the are giving any free items out then distribute it.
2. Announce circles and sessions and what time they each begin.
3. Announce the hacker pledge:
	1. Don’t be a menace!
	2. Treat the space as if you would treat it your own (so that means cleaning up your trash and putting chairs back where you found them)!
	3. Create something, work on something, make something and share it with everyone during checkpoints!
4. Lastly, quickly remind people by announcing hack night ends at **5:00 AM** or whenever the last person is here
## At Every Hour
At every hour such as **9:00, 10:00, and 11:00 PM** announce **5** minutes prior to the session:
1. Session’s Name
2. What it is about
3. Who is leading it
## Countdown
At **11:50 PM **start calling students downstairs. At exactly **11:58 PM** start countdown. See <mention-page url="https://app.notion.com/p/41b181f3b6ed802ebbeef53e151801a9">Countdown</mention-page> for more information.
## Checkpoints
Checkpoints is an integral part to Hack Night. See <mention-page url="https://app.notion.com/p/41b181f3b6ed804b8a78ffab6c799709">Checkpoints</mention-page>
Call up each person to checkpoint to start off with and after each person is done. Be sure to compliment and tell them how interesting their project was. At the end when there are no more people in the queue ask and encourage for any more people to checkpoint. If there are no more then announce the end of checkpoints and say Hack Night goes till whenever the last person is here.
