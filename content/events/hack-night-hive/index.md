title: "Hack Night (HIVE)"
---

Hack Night is a weekly event run on Friday nights at the HIVE (BHEE 270 & 280). It is a time for members to come together, work on projects, and socialize with other creators. Hack Night is open to everyone on campus.
## Schedule
Hack Night has a slightly strict schedule, with members working on projects and socializing. There are some events that are run between 8:00 PM until 11:30 PM. Hack Night has a soft ending time of 3am-5am when we perform teardown, but officially ends when the last person leaves.
- **8:00 PM**: Hack Night starts
- **8:30 PM**: Attendees gather downstairs and Kickoff is performed
- **9:00 PM**: First Session
- **10:00 PM**: First Session
- **11:00 PM**: Second Session
- **11:30 PM**: Checkpoint preparations
- **11:59 PM**: Countdown to Midnight
- **12:00 AM:** Checkpoints
- **2:00 - 5:00 AM**: Teardown
