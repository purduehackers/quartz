title: "Attendance"
---

Tracking attendance is an important part of Hack Night. It helps us keep track of how many members are attending each week, a crucial metric in understanding the health of the event, advertising to sponsors, and more.
## How to Track Attendance
A good way to track overall attendance is to count how many badges there were at the beginning of hack night compared to the end. Another way is to count how many people are at each table group downstairs (4-10 people usually per table).
