---

# Materials
- HDMI cable (≥6 feet)
- BIDC PC
- USB mouse & keyboard (should be present at the PC or one nearby)
# Steps
1. *Get the PC hooked up to the projector. *Connect the HDMI cable to both the A/V podium and the nearest PC. Make sure the end going into the PC goes into the GPU and not the other port on the motherboard (it should be next to the DisplayPort cable for the monitor).
	1. Make sure the projector’s (grey) HDMI cable (which is in the cable bundle in the back of the podium) is plugged into the adapter attached to the top of the podium.
2. Turn on the monitor connected to the PC and set it to the DisplayPort input if it isn’t already selected.
3. Turn on the PC and log in using your Purdue credentials.
4. Turn on the projector using the podium controls if it isn’t already on.
5. Open a web browser. Move the window to the projector screen.
6. Go to [https://night.purduehackers.com](https://night.purduehackers.com).
7. Press `F11` to enter full screen.
8. Zoom in to 150% (use `Ctrl`-`+`).
9. Dismiss the activation popup.
10. Ensure the PC volume is set to 100%. Use the podium volume controls to set the volume to a reasonable level.
11. From another device, open [https://night.purduehackers.com/doorbell](https://night.purduehackers.com/doorbell) and press the doorbell to ensure it works and is audible at a reasonable volume (see previous step).
