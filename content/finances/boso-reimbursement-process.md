---
title: "BOSO Reimbursement Process"
---

The current pipeline for reimbursement is this:
1. People put their receipts in `#receipts`  on Discord. Receipts should have an itemized list of items purchased for what price, date of purchase, and the method of purchase (credit card, PayPal, etc.)
2. If a receipt doesn’t have any of the items that are listed in #1, the treasurer will message the person and ask them to submit a receipt with all items.
3. The treasurer downloads each receipt and converts the ones that are images to JPGs (jpg, not jpeg, because the portal doesn’t recognize the extra ‘e’). PDF receipts are fine, no change is needed.
4. the treasurer logs into the BOSO portal and goes to the Banking section of the Hackers account. They click the button for creating a new reimbursement.
5. They select the person they are doing the reimbursement for. If the person hasn’t been added to the system, the treasurer clicks add new user, and they fill in the information about the user. The treasurer gets this information by discord dming the person asking for this exact info:
	1. full legal name<br>address, with zipcode<br>purdue email<br>phone number
	2. The treasurer then fills in all this info to create the user.
6. The treasurer adds each receipt as a line on the reimbursement form. The memo for the check is a summary of all items being reimbursed.
7. After filling in all the items and clicking continue, the treasurer goes to the receipts section and uploads each JPG/PDF receipt, giving a description of each receipt so BOSO can match each receipt to a line item in the reimbursement. Then hit submit, and wait for BOSO to approve or deny the user/reimbursement
