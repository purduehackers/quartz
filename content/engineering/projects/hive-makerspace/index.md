title: "HIVE Makerspace"
---

Primarily intended to support people doing hardware prototyping, which means the space must have the tools/equipment for manufacturing, electronics, assembly, and more.
<page url="https://app.notion.com/p/3e4181f3b6ed80e0ad0de2798e624799">Items for purchasing</page>
## Requirements:
Assembly Space: Work tables for people to work on/collaborate on projects<br><br>Manufacturing: Rapid prototyping of 3d prints and sheet metal components<br><br>Storage: Lockers for users’ projects, Material storage, and tools/equipment space
## Machines/Equipment
3D printer rack: 
- 3x Prusa Core One: Fast 3D printers intended for majority of 3d prints and batch printing
- 1x Bambu Lab H2D: Large 3D prints, and complex multimaterial prints
Laser cutter:
- Xtool P2: Cut 2d geometries out of wood, acrylic, foam, and other non-metal materials
- Needs an air purifier
Waterjet:
- Wazer Desktop: Manufacture metal parts much more quickly than CNC machines
Electronics Equipment:
- Soldering Irons: General electronics work
- Hot air station and Hot plate: PCB assembly
- Various Crimpers
All tools/equipment (with purchasing links): [Items for purchasing](https://app.notion.com/p/purduehackers/Items-for-purchasing-3e4181f3b6ed80e0ad0de2798e624799?source=copy_link)
## Layout:
Goal: Maximize table space and storage, while keeping the necessary space for machines/equipment and easy accessibility.
### Room:
15' 9" x 20' 7”<br>Outlet locations:
- 24 outlets along the left wall
- 2 outlets on all other walls

## Usage:
- Users must first learn general policies: safety, storage, usage, hours
- Needs to be trained to use machines like a laser cutter and a waterjet (essentially any expensive/complicated equipment)
- Other tools can be used without specific training
3d printers:
- Run by trusted users/manager to ensure efficient operation
## Safety
- On the first table next to the door, safety glasses in a bin are provided and are required when students are using:
	- Dremel, drills, or soldering equipment
- No loose clothing/hair
- Broken machines should be unplugged and have clear labels indicating non-functionality
## Organization/Maintenance 
- Users must keep machines clean and leave tools in correct locations
	- Ex: Remove all material from laser cutters, and place hand tools back in drawers
- All hand tools have a specified location, and drawers have labels to make it easier for tools to be returned correctly
- Maintenance/Repairs are done by the makerspace manager:
	- Maintenance is done on the schedule recommended by the machine manufacturer
## Stock Materials / Consumables
Materials for students to use that will need to be replaced over time. Large projects that use a significant amount of material should be discussed with the people running the makerspace.
- 3D printers: Buy bulk PLA, and occasionally other materials based on demand
- Laser cuttter/waterjet: Stock sheets of common materials such as wood, acrylic, and aluminum 
- Electronics: Solder, flux, desoldering wire, crimps, wire, and general electronics components
- Assembly: Fasteners, types of glue, and sandpaper
- Cleaning: IPA, paper towels
