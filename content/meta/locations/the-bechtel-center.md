title: "The Bechtel Center"
---

The Bechtel Center is located at [1090 3rd Street, West Lafayette, IN 47907](https://maps.app.goo.gl/hvhV2iPDMjKqTMEp8). We currently use the Bechtel Center as our venue for Hack Night.
The Bechtel Center is operated independently from Purdue, therefore we must use their systems and processes to make space reservations, get access to resources, etc.
