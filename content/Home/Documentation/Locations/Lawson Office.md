The Lawson Office is located at [305 N. University St. West Lafayette, IN 47907](https://maps.app.goo.gl/3BKiMyRGdnkmLqBA8). The office is located on the Basement floor, Room B128.
The office is currently co-habitated with the [Purdue Linux Users Group (PLUG)](https://purduelug.org/). We share the space with them and have a few desks and a whiteboard.
The office is secured with Lawson keyfob access. To get access, an Organizer (usually @Lillith) will need to contact the Lawson Mailroom ([workstudy@cs.purdue.edu](mailto:workstudy@cs.purdue.edu)) with the requestor’s name and alias (\<alias\>@purdue.edu) to get a keyfob for them. The office is open 24/7 with keyfob access, and the building is open after-hours with keyfob access. See the [Purdue USB](https://purdueusb.com/wiki/lawson-fob) guide for more information.
