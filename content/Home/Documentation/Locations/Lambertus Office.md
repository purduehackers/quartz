The Lambertus Office is located at [363 Grant St, West Lafayette, IN 47907](https://maps.app.goo.gl/NywBrSSjAKmT2EzSA). The office is located on the first floor, Room 1240.
We don't fully own the space, as it is infrequently used by corporate partners for interviews. They may use the room on Tuesdays, Wednesdays, and Thursdays, and they're typically out by the afternoon. We will be emailed on days when they have to use the room.
We have a few desks (with one swivel chair and two standalone) and a whiteboard in the room. We also have a cabinet that we can use to store our items long-term, since we aren't allowed to put items on the desk.
The office is secured with PUID swipe access. To get access, an Organizer (usually @Sriram) will need to contact [Julie Peretin](https://www.opp.purdue.edu/OPP/about/Staff/Administration/Peretin) (jperetin@purdue.edu) with the requestor’s name and PUID to get swipe access on their behalf. The office is accessible until 9pm with your ID (so long as the<br>building is open).
