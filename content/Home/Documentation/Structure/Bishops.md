---
title: "Bishops"
---

Hack Bishops are pseudo-organizers who help out with the club's operations, specifically around Hack Night.  They are not obligated to attend meetings nor are they required to work on any specific project. They are just individuals who are excited about the club!
Hack Bishops are a way for a person to become an organizer. It is not an obligation for someone to be an organizer if they do not want to become one; however, it is a good way to determine if someone will be a good fit as an organizer if they choose to become a Hack Bishop.
